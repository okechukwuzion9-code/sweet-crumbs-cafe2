No. 06  —  Our story

It started with one oven and a very early alarm.

Sweet Crumbs opened in 2019, when Nadia Osei traded a decade of restaurant pastry kitchens for a single-oven shopfront on Mill Lane. The plan was simple: bake fewer things, bake them properly, and sell out most days by early afternoon.

Every loaf starts the night before as a slow, naturally leavened dough. The croissants are laminated over three days. Nothing is trucked in frozen — if it's in the case, it went into the oven that morning, often before the street outside has properly woken up.

What started as a bread-and-croissant counter has grown into a proper corner café: six tables, a espresso machine Nadia is quietly proud of, and regulars who've been coming since the first week. We're still small on purpose — big enough for a good queue on Saturday, small enough that we still know what you usually order.
